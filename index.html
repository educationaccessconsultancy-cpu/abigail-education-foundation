🔄 How It Works

One Book. One Child. One Future.

Your purchase becomes their possibility. Here is how every e-book you buy transforms a child's life.

"Imagine a child walking barefoot to school, ashamed, head down, hoping no one notices. Now imagine that same child, a few days later, stepping proudly through the school gates — shoes on, uniform crisp, bag on their back, ready to learn. That transformation begins with one simple act: you buying an e-book."

At AESF, we do not collect donations. Instead, we have built something more meaningful — a bridge between your love for reading and a child's right to education. Every e-book purchased through our platform directly funds school shoes, socks, uniforms, bags, stationery, and sanitary pads for a child in need.

To bridge educational access gaps by providing practical school support.

We believe every child has the right to quality education, regardless of their background or circumstances. Our foundation works tirelessly to remove barriers that prevent children from accessing education.

AESF is committed to providing essential educational resources, mentorship, and support to underserved communities, ensuring no child is left behind.

Every child is a person of worth and dignity, deserving of respect, care, and equal opportunity to thrive and reach their full potential.

📚 Education as a Right

Education is not a privilege but a fundamental right that empowers children to build better futures for themselves and their communities.

Who we are

Abigail Education Access Foundation (AEAF) is a humanitarian and educational initiative committed to removing barriers that prevent children and young people from accessing quality education.

The Foundation believes that poverty should never determine a child's educational future. Through strategic partnerships, community engagement, and sustainable educational programmes, AEAF works to ensure that vulnerable learners receive the resources, guidance, and opportunities necessary to succeed.

Our work integrates education support, school health, digital learning, child protection, and community empowerment to improve educational outcomes throughout Tanzania and beyond.

Vision

To create a society where every child, regardless of economic or social background, has equitable access to quality education and the opportunity to realise their full potential.

Mission

To expand access to education through scholarships, learning resources, school supplies, menstrual hygiene support, digital education, academic guidance, community partnerships, and evidence-based educational programmes that promote lifelong learning and social transformation.

Guiding principles

  • Equal educational opportunity for every learner
  • Child safeguarding and protection
  • Integrity, transparency, and accountability
  • Gender equality and inclusion
  • Respect for diversity and human dignity
  • Community participation and local ownership
  • Sustainable educational development
  • Evidence-based planning and continuous improvement

Strategic programme areas

School shoes & uniformsExercise books & textbooksSchool bags & stationeryMenstrual hygiene educationSanitary pad distributionScholarships & sponsorshipCambridge & national curriculum supportHomeschooling & digital educationTeacher developmentEducational researchCareer guidance & university admissionsDigital literacy & technology accessEmergency education assistance

Partnership policy

The Foundation welcomes collaboration with government institutions, schools, universities, faith-based organisations, development partners, NGOs, corporate organisations, community groups, and individual donors and volunteers.

All partnerships shall uphold transparency, child protection, ethical conduct, and responsible stewardship of resources.

Our promise

  • Ensuring every donation creates measurable educational impact
  • Maintaining accountability in programme implementation
  • Protecting every child's dignity and safety
  • Promoting innovation in education
  • Supporting girls' education and menstrual dignity
  • Providing equitable access to learning opportunities
  • Strengthening families through educational support
  • Building sustainable partnerships for long-term impact

Long-term goals

  • Expand educational access across Tanzania
  • Support vulnerable learners from early childhood through higher education
  • Increase digital learning opportunities
  • Strengthen school-community partnerships
  • Improve educational quality through research and innovation
  • Promote inclusive education for children with diverse learning needs
  • Develop future leaders through mentorship and academic excellence

Education transforms lives. Every contribution — whether financial, material, professional, or voluntary — helps remove barriers to learning and creates lasting opportunities for children and young people.
